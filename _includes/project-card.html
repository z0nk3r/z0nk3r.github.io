TWO deliberate departures from post-card.html, both load-bearing: 1. NOT wrapped in one big . A project carries several action links, and an must never nest inside another - the browser silently auto-closes the outer one and the layout breaks (already hit once here). So .project-card__inner takes the layout role .post-card-link normally plays, the title is the primary link, and the action links get their own row. The hover treatment is unaffected: .post-card's brackets and glow are keyed off the
